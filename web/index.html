Echocardiographic measurements & hemodynamic calculations

Research and education only. This interface calculates Doppler-derived estimates and does not establish diagnoses, diastolic grades, pulmonary hypertension, or treatment decisions. Enter synthetic/non-identifiable measurements only.

Calculated results

LVOT area = π(d/2)²; stroke volume = area × VTI; cardiac output = SV × HR / 1000.

Enter measurements and select Calculate.

Interpretation limits

PHT-derived mitral valve area is context-dependent. Estimated RVSP = 4 × TR² + RAP assumes no RV outflow obstruction; it is not a diagnosis of pulmonary hypertension. E/A and E/e-prime are descriptive ratios, not sufficient for diastolic grading or HFpEF diagnosis.
